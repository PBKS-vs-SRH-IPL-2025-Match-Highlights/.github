# PBKS vs SRH IPL 2025 – Match Highlights, Key Moments, and Analysis

*Posted on April 13, 2025 | By [Anand / a cricket enthusiast with over 6 years of covering IPL matches]*

The IPL 2025 clash between Punjab Kings (PBKS) and Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH) at the Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium in Hyderabad was nothing short of a run-fest! SRH chased down a mammoth 246-run target set by PBKS, thanks to Abhishek Sharma’s record-breaking 141 off 55 balls. Want to know how SRH pulled off this historic victory? Let’s dive into the match highlights, key performances, and what this means for both teams in IPL 2025.

## Match Overview: PBKS vs SRH IPL 2025

On April 12, 2025, PBKS elected to bat first after captain Shreyas Iyer won the toss. The Kings posted a formidable 245/6, powered by Iyer’s blazing 82 off 36 balls and a late cameo from Marcus Stoinis. However, SRH’s openers, Abhishek Sharma and Travis Head, turned the game on its head with a 171-run partnership, leading to an 8-wicket victory with 9 balls to spare. This chase is now the second-highest in IPL history!

## Key Moments That Defined PBKS vs SRH

Every IPL match has its turning points, and PBKS vs SRH was packed with them. Here’s what stood out:

- **PBKS’ Explosive Powerplay**: PBKS openers Prabhsimran Singh (42 off 23) and Priyansh Arya (36 off 13) set the tone, racing to 89/1 in 6 overs—one of their best powerplay performances ever. Prabhsimran’s aggressive strokeplay against Mohammed Shami (who leaked 75 runs in 4 overs) gave PBKS early momentum.
- **Shreyas Iyer’s Masterclass**: The PBKS captain smashed 6 fours and 6 sixes, anchoring the innings with a 73-run stand alongside Nehal Wadhera. His quickfire 82 kept PBKS on track for a 250+ total until Harshal Patel’s 4/42 pegged them back.
- **Stoinis’ Final Over Blitz**: Marcus Stoinis smashed four consecutive sixes off Shami in the last over, pushing PBKS to 245/6. This late surge seemed to tilt the game in their favor—until SRH’s chase began.
- **Abhishek Sharma’s Record-Breaking Ton**: Chasing 246, SRH’s Abhishek Sharma played the innings of his life. His 40-ball century (141 off 55, with 14 fours and 10 sixes) broke KL Rahul’s record for the highest individual score by an Indian in IPL history. He dedicated the knock to SRH’s “Orange Army” with a heartfelt note.
- **Travis Head’s Support Act**: Head’s 66 off 37 complemented Abhishek perfectly, ensuring SRH stayed ahead of the required rate. Their 171-run opening stand is the highest partnership of IPL 2025 so far.
- **SRH’s Clinical Finish**: Despite losing Abhishek and Head, Heinrich Klaasen and Ishan Kishan sealed the chase with 9 balls remaining, showcasing SRH’s batting depth.

## Player of the Match: Abhishek Sharma

No surprises here—Abhishek Sharma’s 141 was a game-changer. His fearless strokeplay, including a helicopter shot off Marco Jansen and a 75-meter six off Yash Thakur, left PBKS bowlers shell-shocked. This knock not only won him the Player of the Match award but also etched his name in IPL record books as the third-highest individual scorer ever, behind only Chris Gayle and Brendon McCullum.

> *“This one’s for the Orange Army,”* Abhishek said, waving a note to the Hyderabad crowd after his century.

## What Worked for SRH?

SRH’s victory wasn’t just about Abhishek’s brilliance. Here’s why they dominated:

- **Aggressive Batting Approach**: SRH’s intent was clear from ball one, with Abhishek and Head smashing 83/0 in the powerplay. Their “go hard or go home” strategy paid off on a batting-friendly Hyderabad pitch.
- **Harshal Patel’s Bowling**: While SRH’s bowlers struggled (Shami’s 0/75 was a low point), Harshal Patel’s 4 wickets, including Iyer and Glenn Maxwell, kept PBKS from crossing 260.
- **Team Confidence**: After four straight losses, SRH needed this win to boost morale. Captain Pat Cummins’ faith in his top order to chase big totals was vindicated.

## Where Did PBKS Falter?

Despite their massive total, PBKS couldn’t defend 245. Here’s what went wrong:

- **Bowling Disarray**: Arshdeep Singh and Yash Thakur couldn’t contain SRH’s openers, leaking runs early. Yuzvendra Chahal’s dropped catch of Ab754 proved costly.
- **Injury Blow**: Lockie Ferguson’s hip injury mid-spell disrupted PBKS’ bowling plans, forcing Stoinis to complete his over.
- **Missed Opportunities**: PBKS failed to capitalize on two dropped chances of Abhishek, allowing him to wreak havoc.

## PBKS vs SRH Head-to-Head in IPL

Historically, SRH have dominated PBKS, winning 16 of their 23 IPL encounters before this match. At Hyderabad’s Rajiv Gandhi Stadium, SRH’s record is even stronger, with 8 wins in 9 games against PBKS. This latest victory further cements SRH’s edge in the rivalry.
